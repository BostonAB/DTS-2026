Mitsubishi Outlander

The Mitsubishi Outlander is a mid-size SUV produced by Mitsubishi Motors. First introduced in 2001, it was designed to combine the practicality of an SUV with everyday comfort and efficiency. Over several generations, the Outlander has grown in size and technology, offering features such as three-row seating, advanced safety systems, all-wheel drive, and modern infotainment. It has become one of Mitsubishi’s most important global models.

The current Outlander focuses on a more refined, family-oriented design, with a spacious interior, modern styling, and a range of driver-assistance technologies. Mitsubishi also offers the Outlander PHEV, a plug-in hybrid version that combines a petrol engine with electric motors, allowing shorter trips to be completed using electric power while retaining the flexibility of a petrol engine for longer journeys.
