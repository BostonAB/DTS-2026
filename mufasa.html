Hyundai Mufasa

The Hyundai Mufasa is a compact SUV introduced in 2023, developed primarily for the Chinese market through Hyundai’s joint venture with Beijing Automotive Group. It features a distinctive exterior design, with bold front styling, angular body lines and a modern rear light arrangement. Positioned as a practical family SUV, the Mufasa combines a compact footprint with a spacious interior, making it suitable for urban driving and everyday use.

The Mufasa is generally equipped with a 2.0-litre naturally aspirated petrol engine and a six-speed automatic transmission, producing around 118 kW of power, depending on the market. Its cabin offers modern infotainment, comfortable seating and available driver-assistance and safety technologies. Overall, the Hyundai Mufasa is designed to provide a balance of distinctive styling, interior space, everyday comfort and value for money, making it a practical option for small families and urban drivers.
