Mitsubishi Grandis

The Mitsubishi Grandis is a multi-purpose vehicle (MPV) produced by Mitsubishi Motors. Introduced in 2003, it was designed as a spacious family vehicle with seating for up to seven passengers. The Grandis combined the practicality of a people mover with a more stylish and car-like design, making it suitable for families and longer journeys.

The Grandis offered a comfortable and versatile interior, with flexible seating arrangements that allowed owners to increase passenger space or cargo capacity as needed. It was available with different petrol and diesel engines depending on the market and was sold in several countries. Production of the Grandis ended in 2011, but it remains remembered for its combination of family practicality, generous interior space, and distinctive styling.
