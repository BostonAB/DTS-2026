Mitsubishi Mirage

The Mitsubishi Mirage is a small, economical hatchback developed by Mitsubishi Motors. First introduced in 1978, the Mirage has appeared in several generations, with the modern version being designed primarily as an affordable and fuel-efficient city car. Its compact size makes it easy to drive and park, while its relatively low running costs have made it popular with budget-conscious drivers.

The modern Mirage focuses on simplicity, practicality, and efficiency rather than performance. It typically features a small petrol engine, a lightweight body, and a compact interior that can accommodate everyday passengers and luggage. With its affordable price and economical operation, the Mirage has been positioned as an accessible entry-level vehicle for urban and everyday driving.
