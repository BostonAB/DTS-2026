Hyundai STARIA

The Hyundai STARIA is a multi-purpose vehicle (MPV) introduced in 2021, designed to provide spacious, comfortable and modern transport for families, businesses and passenger services. It stands out with its futuristic exterior styling, featuring a streamlined body, a wide front design and large windows that create a bright, open cabin. Depending on the market and configuration, the STARIA is available with different seating arrangements, accommodating between two and eleven occupants, making it suitable for both personal and commercial use.

The STARIA offers a range of petrol and diesel engines, with hybrid versions also available in selected markets. Its interior focuses on comfort and practicality, with flexible seating, generous storage, modern infotainment technology and advanced safety features. Higher-specification models provide premium finishes and additional convenience features, while commercial versions are designed for carrying passengers or cargo. Overall, the Hyundai STARIA combines distinctive styling, versatility and space, making it a practical choice for large families, shuttle services and businesses.
