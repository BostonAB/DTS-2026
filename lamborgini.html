Brief History on Lamborgini:

Automobili Lamborghini was founded in 1963 by Italian industrialist and tractor manufacturer Ferruccio Lamborghini in Sant'Agata Bolognese, Italy, driven by his desire to build a refined sports car that could rival Ferrari.
