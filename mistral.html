Bugatti Mistral

The Bugatti W16 Mistral was unveiled in 2022 as Bugatti’s ultimate open-top hypercar and the final road-going model to use its legendary W16 engine. It features an 8.0-litre quad-turbocharged W16 engine producing 1,600 PS (1,578 horsepower), combining extreme performance with elegant styling and a luxurious interior.

Only 99 examples were produced, making the Mistral an exceptionally rare car. In November 2024, a specially prepared version reached 453.91 km/h (282 mph), setting a world record for an open-top car. Production concluded in 2026, marking the end of Bugatti’s road-going W16 era and the beginning of a new chapter for the brand.
