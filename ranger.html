Ford Ranger

The Ford Ranger is a mid-size pickup truck produced by Ford and is one of the company’s most popular utility vehicles. Originally introduced in 1983, the Ranger has gone through several generations, becoming larger, more powerful, and more technologically advanced over time. It is designed for both work and everyday driving, offering a spacious cabin, a practical cargo bed, and strong towing and payload capabilities. The Ranger is particularly popular in markets such as Australia and New Zealand.

The modern Ford Ranger is available with several engine options, including turbocharged petrol and diesel engines, depending on the market. Four-wheel-drive models are designed for off-road use and can handle challenging terrain, while features such as selectable drive modes, advanced safety systems, and modern infotainment improve its everyday usability. High-performance versions such as the Ranger Raptor add powerful engines and specialized suspension for off-road driving, making the Ranger a versatile vehicle for work, recreation, and adventure.
