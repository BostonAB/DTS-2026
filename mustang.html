Ford Mustang

The Ford Mustang is an iconic American sports car first introduced by Ford in 1964. It quickly became popular because it combined sporty styling, strong performance, and an affordable price compared with many other sports cars. Over the years, the Mustang has been produced in many generations and configurations, including powerful V8 models such as the Mustang GT, as well as more economical versions. Its distinctive design and performance helped establish the “pony car” category and made the Mustang one of the most recognizable cars in the world.

Today, the Mustang continues to combine classic muscle-car characteristics with modern technology, safety features, and performance. The current generation offers models ranging from everyday versions to high-performance variants, including the Mustang Dark Horse. Despite changing significantly over the decades, the Mustang has retained its focus on performance, rear-wheel drive, and distinctive styling, while becoming more technologically advanced and refined.
