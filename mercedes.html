Brief History on Mercedes:

Mercedes-Benz traces its roots to 1886 when Carl Benz patented the world's first gasoline-powered car (the Patent-Motorwagen) and Gottlieb Daimler developed a motorized carriage. The iconic brand was officially born in June 1926 through the merger of Benz & Cie. and Daimler-Motoren-Gesellschaft (DMG).
