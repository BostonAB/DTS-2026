Bugatti Dico

The Bugatti Divo was unveiled in 2018 as a limited-production hypercar based on the Bugatti Chiron. It features an 8.0-litre quad-turbocharged W16 engine producing 1,500 horsepower, with a focus on improved handling, cornering, and aerodynamics rather than maximum top speed. Its aggressive styling, lightweight components, and enhanced downforce make it especially suited to winding tracks.

Only 40 Bugatti Divos were produced, making it an extremely rare and exclusive car. Named after French racing driver Albert Divo, the car pays tribute to Bugatti’s motorsport heritage while combining exceptional performance with luxurious craftsmanship.
