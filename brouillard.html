Bugatti Brouillard

The Bugatti Brouillard was unveiled in 2025 as a unique, one-of-a-kind hypercar and the first creation of Bugatti’s exclusive Programme Solitaire. Inspired by Ettore Bugatti’s favourite horse, it features an elegant green exterior, sculpted bodywork, and a handcrafted interior decorated with horse-themed details.

Powered by an 8.0-litre quad-turbocharged W16 engine producing 1,600 PS, the Brouillard combines extreme performance with bespoke luxury and craftsmanship. Its custom design celebrates Bugatti’s heritage of coachbuilding, making it a unique blend of automotive engineering and artistic expression.
