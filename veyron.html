Bugatti Veyron

The Bugatti Veyron is a luxury hypercar introduced in 2005 by Bugatti under Volkswagen Group ownership. It was designed to combine incredible speed with comfort and advanced engineering. Powered by an 8.0-litre quad-turbocharged W16 engine, the original Veyron produced 1,001 horsepower and could reach a top speed of approximately 407 km/h, making it one of the fastest production cars in the world at the time.

The Veyron became a landmark in automotive history because of its groundbreaking performance, distinctive design, and enormous engineering costs. Its success helped restore Bugatti’s reputation as a leading manufacturer of exclusive hypercars and paved the way for its successor, the Bugatti Chiron.
