Brief History on Bugatti:

Bugatti was founded in 1909 by Ettore Bugatti in Molsheim, France. The company became famous for its luxurious designs, innovative engineering, and successful racing cars, especially the Type 35. However, following the death of Ettore’s son in 1939 and the effects of World War II, the company struggled and eventually stopped producing cars. Bugatti was revived in 1987, producing the EB110 supercar before financial difficulties forced it to close in 1995. Volkswagen acquired the brand in 1998, leading to the creation of the legendary Veyron and Chiron hypercars. In 2021, Bugatti joined Rimac, beginning a new chapter focused on high-performance engineering and future technologies.
