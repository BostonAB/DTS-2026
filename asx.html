Mitsubishi ASX

The Mitsubishi ASX is a compact SUV produced by Mitsubishi Motors and first introduced in 2010. Known as the Outlander Sport in some markets, the ASX was designed to offer the practicality and higher driving position of an SUV while remaining relatively compact and affordable. Over its production life, it received several updates to its styling, technology, safety equipment, and powertrains.

The ASX has been particularly popular with drivers looking for a practical vehicle for everyday city and suburban use. Depending on the model and market, it has offered features such as touchscreen infotainment, smartphone connectivity, advanced safety systems, and efficient petrol engines. Its combination of compact dimensions, SUV styling, and practical interior has made the ASX an important model in Mitsubishi's range.
