Ford Equator

The Ford Equator is a mid-size SUV developed by Ford in partnership with Jiangling Motors (JMC) and introduced primarily for the Chinese market in 2021. It was designed as a spacious family SUV, with three rows of seating and a focus on comfort, practicality, and modern technology. The Equator features a bold exterior design, a large interior, and features such as a digital instrument display and touchscreen infotainment system.

The Equator is powered by a 2.0-litre turbocharged petrol engine and is available with front-wheel drive or, depending on the version, four-wheel drive. Ford later introduced the Equator Sport, a smaller and more compact version. Unlike globally sold Ford models such as the Mustang and Explorer, the Equator has primarily been marketed in China and selected international markets.
