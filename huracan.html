Lamborghini Huracán

The Lamborghini Huracán is a mid-engine Italian supercar that was introduced in 2014 as the successor to the Lamborghini Gallardo. It is powered by a naturally aspirated 5.2-litre V10 engine, with different versions producing between approximately 580 and 640 horsepower. The Huracán is known for its sharp styling, excellent handling, and high-revving V10 engine. Depending on the variant, it can accelerate from 0–100 km/h in around 2.9–3.4 seconds and reach a top speed of more than 320 km/h.

Throughout its production, Lamborghini released several versions of the Huracán, including the EVO, STO, Performante, Tecnica, and Sterrato. These models ranged from comfortable road-focused versions to highly specialised track cars and even an off-road-inspired variant. The Huracán became one of Lamborghini's most successful modern models, combining supercar performance with a relatively approachable driving experience. Production ended in 2024, with the Huracán's successor moving Lamborghini further toward hybrid technology.
