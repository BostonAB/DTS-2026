Bugatti Bolide

The Bugatti Bolide was unveiled in 2020 as an extreme, track-only hypercar designed to push the limits of speed and performance. It features an 8.0-litre quad-turbocharged W16 engine producing up to 1,600 PS, combined with an incredibly lightweight body, aggressive aerodynamics, and advanced cooling systems. Its design prioritises maximum downforce, rapid cornering, and exceptional acceleration rather than everyday comfort.

Bugatti announced a limited production run of just 40 Bolides, making it a rare and exclusive track machine. Unlike road-legal Bugattis such as the Chiron, the Bolide was engineered specifically for racing circuits, drawing inspiration from motorsport technology. It represents one of Bugatti’s most extreme creations and showcases the full potential of its legendary W16 engine.
