Ford Puma

The Ford Puma is a compact crossover SUV produced by Ford and first introduced in its modern form in 2019. It was designed primarily for urban and everyday driving, combining the higher seating position and practicality of an SUV with the compact size and handling of a small car. The Puma features a sporty exterior design, a practical interior, and a surprisingly spacious cargo area for its size.

The Puma is offered with efficient turbocharged petrol engines, with some versions using mild-hybrid technology to improve fuel efficiency. It also includes modern features such as touchscreen infotainment, driver-assistance systems, and smartphone connectivity. A performance-focused Puma ST version was also produced, offering a more powerful engine and sportier suspension. Overall, the Ford Puma is aimed at drivers looking for a small, practical SUV that is economical and enjoyable to drive.
