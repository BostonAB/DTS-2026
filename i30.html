Hyundai i30

The Hyundai i30 is a compact hatchback introduced by Hyundai in 2007, designed to offer a practical, reliable and affordable vehicle for everyday driving. Developed primarily for the European market, the i30 competes with popular cars such as the Toyota Corolla, Mazda3 and Volkswagen Golf. Over several generations, it has gained features including improved safety technology, modern infotainment systems, fuel-efficient petrol and diesel engines, and a comfortable interior.

The i30 is available in various body styles and specifications, depending on the market, including hatchback and estate versions. Hyundai has also produced sporty performance models under its N sub-brand, such as the i30 N, which offers increased power, sharper handling and a more engaging driving experience. Overall, the i30 is known for its balance of practicality, value for money, comfort and performance, making it a popular choice for individuals and families alike.
