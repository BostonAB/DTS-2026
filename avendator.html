Lamborghini Aventador

The Lamborghini Aventador is a high-performance Italian supercar that was produced from 2011 to 2022 as Lamborghini’s flagship model. It is powered by a naturally aspirated 6.5-litre V12 engine, producing between roughly 690 and 770 horsepower depending on the version. The Aventador features Lamborghini’s distinctive sharp and aggressive styling, a lightweight carbon-fibre monocoque, and all-wheel drive. Its performance is exceptional, with many versions capable of accelerating from 0–100 km/h in under 3 seconds and reaching speeds of over 350 km/h.

The Aventador became one of Lamborghini’s most recognisable models and was offered in several variants, including the Aventador S, SV, SVJ, and Ultimae. It combined extreme performance with a dramatic interior, advanced aerodynamics, and Lamborghini’s iconic scissor doors. The model is particularly significant because it was one of the last Lamborghini flagship cars to rely solely on a naturally aspirated V12 engine, before the hybrid-powered Revuelto took its place in 2023.
