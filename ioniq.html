Hyundai IONIQ 3

The Hyundai IONIQ 3 is part of Hyundai’s growing IONIQ electric-vehicle family, which focuses on combining modern design, innovative technology and environmentally friendly driving. Positioned as a smaller electric car for everyday use, it is aimed at drivers who want the benefits of an electric vehicle in a practical, compact package. The IONIQ name is associated with Hyundai’s development of electric mobility, with an emphasis on efficiency, contemporary styling and advanced digital features.

Officially introduced in April 2026, the IONIQ 3 features an aerodynamic hatchback design, a spacious five-seat interior and modern infotainment technology. Hyundai announced two battery options with projected driving ranges of 344 km for the Standard Range and up to 496 km for the Long Range (WLTP estimates). It also supports rapid DC charging, with charging from 10% to 80% taking approximately 29–30 minutes under suitable conditions. Overall, the IONIQ 3 is designed to make electric driving more accessible and practical for everyday commuters and small families.
