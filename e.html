Porsche Mission E

The Porsche Mission E was an all-electric concept car unveiled by Porsche at the 2015 Frankfurt Motor Show. It was designed to demonstrate the future of electric sports cars by combining Porsche’s traditional performance and styling with innovative electric technology. The concept featured four doors, four seats, all-wheel drive, and an electric powertrain producing around 600 horsepower. Porsche claimed it could accelerate from 0 to 100 km/h in under 3.5 seconds and travel more than 500 kilometres on a single charge under the original test cycle.

The Mission E attracted attention for its advanced 800-volt electrical system, which was designed to enable faster charging than many electric vehicles of its time. Its design and technology helped shape Porsche’s plans for electric mobility, and in 2019, the concept became a production vehicle called the Porsche Taycan. Today, the Mission E is remembered as an important milestone in Porsche’s transition towards electric vehicles, showing that the company could combine its sporting heritage with a fully electric future.
