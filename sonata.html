Hyundai Sonata

The Hyundai Sonata is a mid-size sedan first introduced in 1985, helping Hyundai establish itself in the global passenger-car market. Over the years, it has evolved into a stylish and comfortable family sedan, known for its spacious interior, smooth driving experience and competitive pricing. The Sonata has undergone several generations of design and engineering improvements, featuring sleeker styling, more advanced technology and enhanced safety features.

Modern versions of the Sonata offer a range of petrol and hybrid powertrains, depending on the market, along with touchscreen infotainment systems, smartphone connectivity and advanced driver-assistance technologies. Higher-performance variants, such as the N Line, provide increased power and sportier handling. Overall, the Hyundai Sonata combines comfort, efficiency, modern features and value for money, making it a strong competitor in the mid-size sedan segment.
