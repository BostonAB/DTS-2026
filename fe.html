Hyundai Santa Fe

The Hyundai Santa Fe is a mid-size SUV first introduced in 2000, becoming Hyundai’s first SUV and an important step in the company’s expansion into the family vehicle market. Over several generations, it has evolved from a practical, affordable SUV into a more refined vehicle featuring bold exterior styling, a spacious interior and modern technology. The Santa Fe is designed to suit families and drivers who need comfort, versatility and space for everyday use and longer journeys.

The latest generation features a distinctive, boxier design, a large touchscreen infotainment system, advanced safety and driver-assistance features, and seating for up to seven people, depending on the configuration. Engine options vary by market and include petrol, hybrid and, in some markets, plug-in hybrid powertrains. With its flexible seating, generous cargo space and available all-wheel drive, the Hyundai Santa Fe is well suited to family trips and outdoor adventures. Overall, it combines practicality, comfort and modern technology in a versatile SUV package.
