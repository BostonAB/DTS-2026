Brief History on Ferrari:

Founded by ⁠Enzo Ferrari in 1939 as Auto Avio Costruzioni, the iconic Italian brand originally built race cars and aircraft parts. The first true Ferrari-badged road car, the 125 S, debuted in 1947. Enzo built street cars primarily to fund his true passion: competitive racing.
