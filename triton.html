Mitsubishi Triton

The Mitsubishi Triton is a mid-size pickup truck produced by Mitsubishi Motors and has been sold in markets around the world since the late 1970s. Originally developed as a practical work vehicle, the Triton has evolved through several generations into a versatile pickup aimed at both commercial and recreational drivers. It is known for its rugged construction, useful cargo capacity, off-road capability, and available four-wheel drive.

The latest Triton combines its traditional work-focused abilities with greater comfort, technology, and safety. Depending on the market and specification, it offers modern diesel powertrains, advanced four-wheel-drive systems, driver-assistance features, and a more refined cabin. The Triton competes with other popular pickups such as the Toyota Hilux and Ford Ranger and is widely used for work, towing, touring, and off-road driving.
