Brief History on Ford:

Ford Motor Company was founded by Henry Ford in 1903 in Detroit, Michigan. The company became famous for making cars affordable to ordinary people, particularly with the introduction of the Model T in 1908. Ford pioneered the use of moving assembly lines, which greatly increased production speed and helped lower the cost of vehicles. By the 1920s, the Model T had become one of the world's most successful cars. Over the following decades, Ford expanded its range and became one of the world's major automobile manufacturers. It produced iconic vehicles such as the Mustang, introduced in 1964, as well as the F-Series pickup trucks, which became particularly successful in North America. Ford has continued to develop petrol, hybrid, and electric vehicles, including models such as the Mustang Mach-E and F-150 Lightning. Today, Ford remains one of the major global car manufacturers, with a history spanning more than 120 years.
