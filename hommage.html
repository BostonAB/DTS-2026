Bugatti F.K.P Hommage

The Bugatti F.K.P. Hommage is a special one-off hypercar created to honour Ferdinand Karl Piëch, the Volkswagen Group engineer whose vision helped make the Bugatti Veyron possible. It pays tribute to the original Veyron while combining its iconic design with modern styling and engineering.

Its most impressive feature is its 8.0-litre quad-turbocharged W16 engine, producing 1,600 horsepower, alongside a redesigned body, a luxurious custom interior, and a unique watch integrated into the dashboard. Created as a one-off through Bugatti’s exclusive Programme Solitaire, the F.K.P. Hommage celebrates 20 years of the Veyron and represents a blend of historic design, modern performance, and bespoke craftsmanship.
