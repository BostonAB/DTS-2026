Mitsubishi Xforce

The Mitsubishi Xforce is a compact SUV introduced by Mitsubishi Motors in 2023, primarily aimed at markets in Southeast Asia and other emerging regions. It was developed to provide a combination of modern styling, practicality, and urban-friendly dimensions. The Xforce features a spacious interior for its class, a raised driving position, and contemporary technology and safety features.

The Xforce is designed mainly for everyday city and suburban driving, with an emphasis on comfort, efficiency, and practicality rather than serious off-road performance. It typically uses a petrol engine with a continuously variable transmission (CVT), while features can include smartphone connectivity, touchscreen infotainment, advanced driver-assistance systems, and selectable driving modes. Its combination of SUV styling, interior space, and accessible pricing has made it an important newer model in Mitsubishi's global lineup.
