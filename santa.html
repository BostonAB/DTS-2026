Hyundai Santa Cruz

The Hyundai Santa Cruz is a compact sport adventure vehicle introduced by Hyundai in 2021, combining the comfort and styling of an SUV with the practicality of a pickup truck. Designed primarily for the North American market, it features a four-door cabin, a short open cargo bed and a car-like driving experience. The Santa Cruz is aimed at drivers who want the versatility of a truck for carrying equipment and recreational gear without the size of a traditional pickup.

The Santa Cruz is available with petrol engines, including a turbocharged option, and features modern infotainment, driver-assistance systems and available all-wheel drive. Higher-specification versions offer enhanced off-road capability and towing capacity of up to approximately 2,268 kg (5,000 lb), depending on configuration. The 2025 model received significant styling and interior technology updates, including a redesigned dashboard and larger displays. Overall, the Santa Cruz is ideal for people seeking a versatile lifestyle vehicle that combines everyday comfort with the utility needed for outdoor activities and light-duty hauling.
