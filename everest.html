Ford Everest

The Ford Everest is a mid-size SUV developed by Ford primarily for markets in the Asia-Pacific region, including Australia and New Zealand. It was first introduced in 2003 and is closely related to the Ford Ranger, sharing its platform and many mechanical components. The Everest was designed to combine the practicality and comfort of a family SUV with strong off-road capabilities, making it suitable for both everyday driving and adventurous conditions.

The latest generation of the Everest features a modern interior, advanced safety technology, and a range of turbocharged petrol and diesel engines. Its four-wheel-drive variants are designed for challenging terrain, while features such as multiple drive modes, high ground clearance, and towing capability improve its versatility. Overall, the Ford Everest is aimed at drivers who want a spacious and comfortable SUV that can also handle off-road driving, towing, and long-distance travel.
