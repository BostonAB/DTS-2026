Brief History on Porsche:

Porsche was founded in 1931 by Ferdinand Porsche in Stuttgart, Germany, originally as an engineering and vehicle development company. Before producing its own cars, the company designed vehicles and engines for other manufacturers, including helping develop the Volkswagen Beetle. In 1948, Porsche introduced its first car under its own name, the Porsche 356, which became popular for its lightweight design, performance, and distinctive styling. The company later moved its main operations to Stuttgart-Zuffenhausen, where it continues to be based today. In 1964, Porsche launched the Porsche 911, which became its most famous and recognisable model. Known for its rear-mounted engine, sporty handling, and distinctive shape, the 911 helped establish Porsche as a leading sports car manufacturer. Over the following decades, Porsche expanded into motorsport, winning major races such as the 24 Hours of Le Mans, and introduced new models, including the Cayenne SUV and the Panamera luxury sedan. Today, Porsche is a globally recognised brand known for combining performance, engineering innovation, luxury, and racing heritage.
