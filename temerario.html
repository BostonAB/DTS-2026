Lamborghini Temerario

The Lamborghini Temerario is a high-performance hybrid supercar introduced in 2024 as the successor to the Lamborghini Huracán. It represents Lamborghini's move toward electrification while maintaining the brand's focus on extreme performance. The Temerario uses a 4.0-litre twin-turbocharged V8 engine combined with three electric motors, producing a combined 920 horsepower (686 kW). It can accelerate from 0–100 km/h in around 2.7 seconds and has a top speed of over 340 km/h.

The Temerario features Lamborghini's distinctive sharp and aggressive styling, with a lightweight aluminium chassis, advanced aerodynamics, and a driver-focused interior. Its hybrid system provides additional power and instant electric torque while also allowing limited electric-only driving. With advanced technology, a high-revving V8, and an eight-speed dual-clutch transmission, the Temerario is designed to combine the performance of a traditional Lamborghini supercar with the efficiency and flexibility of modern hybrid technology.
