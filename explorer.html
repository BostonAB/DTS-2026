Ford Explorer

The Ford Explorer is a mid-size SUV that has been produced by Ford since 1990. It was originally introduced as a replacement for the Ford Bronco II and became popular for its combination of passenger space, practicality, and off-road capability. Over the years, the Explorer has grown larger and more refined, changing from a traditional body-on-frame SUV to a unibody design. It is available with multiple rows of seating, making it suitable for families and long-distance travel.

Modern versions of the Ford Explorer focus on comfort, technology, and performance, with features such as touchscreen infotainment, advanced driver-assistance systems, and multiple engine options depending on the market. Some versions offer four-wheel drive and selectable driving modes for off-road conditions. The Explorer has also been offered in performance-oriented variants, such as the Explorer ST, which provides increased power and sportier handling.
