Bugatti Centodieci

The Bugatti Centodieci was unveiled in 2019 as a limited-edition hypercar inspired by the iconic Bugatti EB110 from the 1990s. Powered by an 8.0-litre quad-turbocharged W16 engine producing 1,600 horsepower, it can accelerate from 0 to 100 km/h in approximately 2.4 seconds. Its distinctive design, featuring five circular air intakes and a sharp, angular body, pays tribute to Bugatti’s history while incorporating modern engineering.

Only 10 Centodieci cars were produced, making it one of Bugatti’s rarest models, with an original price of around €8 million each before taxes. The Centodieci, meaning “110” in Italian, celebrates 110 years since Bugatti’s founding in 1909 and showcases the brand’s combination of exclusivity, luxury, and extreme performance.
