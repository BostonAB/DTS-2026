Ford Taurus

The Ford Taurus is a mid-size to full-size sedan produced by Ford from 1986 to 2019, with a brief production gap. It became one of Ford’s most important family cars, offering a practical interior, comfortable ride, and relatively affordable price. The Taurus was also notable for its aerodynamic design when it was first introduced, which was quite different from the boxy styling common on American cars at the time. Over its production, Ford offered several generations with different engines, body styles, and technology.

Throughout its history, the Taurus was available in a variety of versions, including the performance-oriented Taurus SHO (Super High Output), which featured more powerful engines and sportier handling. Later generations became larger and more luxurious, competing with other family and executive sedans. Ford discontinued the Taurus in 2019 as consumer demand shifted toward SUVs and crossovers, ending more than three decades of production.
