Brief History on the Automobile:

The history of the automobile began in the late 19th century, with Carl Benz patenting the first gasoline-powered car in 1886. Early cars were expensive, hand-built luxuries, but Henry Ford's Model T (1908) revolutionized the industry with mass production, making cars accessible to the general population. Following decades saw rapid advances in speed, design (such as 1920s streamlining), and safety, turning vehicles from novelties into modern necessities
