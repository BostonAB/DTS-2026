Mitsubishi Delica D:5

The Mitsubishi Delica D:5 is a versatile Japanese minivan introduced in 2007 as the successor to the Delica Space Gear. It was designed to combine the practicality of a family people mover with the rugged capability of an SUV. The D:5 is known for its distinctive boxy styling, spacious interior, high ground clearance, and available four-wheel drive, making it popular with families and outdoor enthusiasts.

The Delica D:5 offers seating for up to eight passengers and has a flexible cabin designed for both passengers and cargo. Its four-wheel-drive versions are particularly well suited to camping, touring, and rougher roads, while modern versions feature improved safety technology, infotainment, and comfort. The Delica D:5 remains primarily associated with the Japanese market and has developed a strong following among people looking for a practical vehicle with genuine off-road capability.
