Brief History on Mitsubishi:

Mitsubishi traces its origins to 1870, when Yataro Iwasaki founded a shipping company in Japan. The business expanded into mining, shipbuilding, banking, and other industries, eventually becoming the Mitsubishi group. Its distinctive three-diamond logo and the name “Mitsubishi” became closely associated with the growing conglomerate. After World War II, Mitsubishi was broken up under Japan’s economic reforms, and its businesses became separate companies. One of these, Mitsubishi Motors, was established as an automobile manufacturer in 1970. Since then, Mitsubishi Motors has become an international car company known for vehicles such as the Lancer, Pajero, and Outlander, while the wider Mitsubishi name continues to be used by numerous independent companies across finance, electronics, heavy industry, and other sectors.
