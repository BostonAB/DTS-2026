Ford Maverick

The Ford Maverick is a compact pickup truck introduced by Ford in 2021. It was designed as a smaller and more affordable alternative to larger trucks such as the Ford Ranger and F-150. The Maverick features a five-seat cabin and a practical truck bed, making it suitable for everyday driving, carrying cargo, and light towing. Its smaller size also makes it easier to drive and park in urban environments compared with full-size pickup trucks.

The Maverick is available with different powertrain options, including a 2.5-litre hybrid and a 2.0-litre turbocharged petrol engine. Depending on the configuration, it can also be equipped with all-wheel drive and additional off-road features. The Maverick has become known for combining the practicality of a pickup truck with the fuel efficiency, size, and affordability of a smaller vehicle, making it particularly appealing to people who do not need a large truck.
