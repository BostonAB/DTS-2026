Lamborghini Revuelto

The Lamborghini Revuelto is a high-performance plug-in hybrid supercar introduced in 2023 as the successor to the Aventador. It combines a naturally aspirated 6.5-litre V12 engine with three electric motors, producing a combined 1,001 horsepower (747 kW). Power is sent through an eight-speed dual-clutch transmission and all-wheel drive, allowing the Revuelto to accelerate from 0–100 km/h in about 2.5 seconds and reach a top speed of over 350 km/h.

The Revuelto combines Lamborghini's traditional V12 character with modern hybrid technology and an aggressive, futuristic design. Its interior features a driver-focused cockpit with multiple digital displays, while the hybrid system can provide limited electric-only driving for short distances. The car uses a lightweight carbon-fibre-intensive structure and advanced aerodynamics to improve performance and handling. Overall, the Revuelto represents Lamborghini's transition toward electrification while retaining the V12 engine that has become a defining feature of the brand's flagship supercars.
