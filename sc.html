Lamborghini SC18

The Lamborghini SC18 Alston is a unique one-off supercar created by Lamborghini’s motorsport division, Squadra Corse, in collaboration with Lamborghini Centro Stile. Unveiled in 2018, it was developed as a highly personalised track-focused car for an individual customer. The SC18 is based on the Aventador SVJ and features an extremely aggressive aerodynamic design, including a large rear wing, prominent front splitter, air intakes, and numerous carbon-fibre components. Its styling incorporates elements inspired by Lamborghini’s racing cars while remaining road-registered.

Underneath its distinctive bodywork, the SC18 uses a 6.5-litre naturally aspirated V12 producing around 770 horsepower (574 kW), paired with a seven-speed automated manual transmission and all-wheel drive. The car was designed primarily with track performance in mind, featuring extensive aerodynamic development and lightweight materials. As a one-off creation, the SC18 demonstrates Lamborghini's ability to produce highly customised vehicles and represents an extreme interpretation of the Aventador platform.
