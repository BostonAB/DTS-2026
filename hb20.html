Hyundai HB20

The Hyundai HB20 is a subcompact car introduced by Hyundai in 2012, developed primarily for the Brazilian market. The name HB20 stands for Hyundai Brasil 20, reflecting its focus on local customers. Available in hatchback and sedan versions, the HB20 was designed to provide affordable, practical and fuel-efficient transport, with modern styling and features suited to everyday driving. It quickly became a popular model in Brazil, helping Hyundai strengthen its presence in South America.

Over the years, the HB20 has received several updates, including refreshed exterior designs, improved interior quality, modern infotainment systems and enhanced safety features. Depending on the model year and market, it has been offered with a range of petrol and flex-fuel engines designed to balance performance and economy. Overall, the Hyundai HB20 is known for its affordability, compact size, practicality and suitability for urban driving, making it a strong competitor in Brazil’s small-car market.
